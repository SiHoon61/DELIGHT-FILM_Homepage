## Design Context

### Users

The public site is for prospective clients evaluating a video production company. They use the Works page to scan the portfolio quickly, compare production styles, and build confidence before making contact. The Admin area is for the internal team managing the content and its presentation.

### Brand Personality

Cinematic, restrained, and professional. The experience should feel confident and editorial without competing with the film work itself.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the existing dark, image-led visual language with quiet typography and high-contrast content. Motion should feel like a controlled film transition: subtle, purposeful, and brief. Avoid playful bounce, loud gradients, decorative effects, and generic dashboard styling.

### Design Principles

1. Let the portfolio imagery remain the primary visual focus.
2. Preserve layout stability while data and media load.
3. Use restrained motion to communicate state, not to decorate.
4. Keep desktop and mobile experiences intentionally composed for their context.
5. Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain clear loading feedback without animation.
